About the Center

The Institute Instrumentation Center (IIC) has been established in 2017 as a centralized, multidisciplinary research facility to support high-quality scientific research, innovation, and technological advancement within the Institute. The Centre provides access to state-of-the-art analytical instruments, advanced manufacturing facilities, and specialized characterization techniques, enabling researchers to undertake cutting-edge research across diverse disciplines of science, engineering, and technology. The primary objective of the Institute Instrumentation Center is to provide world-class research infrastructure and technical expertise to faculty members, research scholars, postgraduate students, and undergraduate students of the Institute. In addition, the Centre extends its analytical, characterization, and testing services to researchers, academic institutions, government organizations, research laboratories, and industries across the country, thereby fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and promoting innovation through research, consultancy, and industrial partnerships.

The Institute Instrumentation Center serves as a common platform for advanced experimentation, product development, materials characterization, process optimization, and technology validation. Through its sophisticated infrastructure and expert technical support, the Centre significantly contributes to strengthening the Institute's research ecosystem and enhancing its academic, research, and industrial outreach.

IIC Objectives:

  • To provide state-of-the-art facilities for materials characterization, modelling and simulation, additive manufacturing, high-resolution imaging, precision chemical analysis, and other advanced research activities.
  • To support high-quality research by offering reliable analytical, characterization, testing, and technical services to both internal and external users.
  • To promote interdisciplinary research and collaboration among academic institutions, research organizations, government agencies, and industries.
  • To facilitate consultancy services, sponsored research, product development, innovation, and technology transfer in collaboration with industrial and research partners.
  • To disseminate knowledge and develop technical skills through training programmes, workshops, seminars, conferences, and short-term courses on advanced instrumentation and emerging technologies.
  • To ensure the efficient operation, maintenance, and optimum utilization of sophisticated research equipment while adhering to established quality, safety, and operational standards.

The Institute Instrumentation Center is committed to fostering a culture of research excellence by providing world-class facilities, technical expertise, and collaborative opportunities that support innovation, scientific discovery, and technological advancement for the benefit of academia, industry, and society.

Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometer (ICP-MS/MS)

(01 Nos)

The Agilent 8900 Triple Quadrupole Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometer (ICP-MS/MS) is a state-of-the-art analytical instrument designed for ultra-trace elemental analysis with exceptional accuracy, sensitivity, and reliability. Equipped with tandem mass spectrometry (ICP-MS/MS) technology, the system provides superior interference removal and precise quantification of trace and ultra-trace elements in complex sample matrices. Its advanced collision/reaction cell enables highly selective analysis, making it suitable for cutting-edge research, environmental monitoring, materials characterization, geochemical studies, biological sciences, pharmaceutical analysis, and high-purity material testing.

The instrument combines high analytical performance with excellent matrix tolerance, allowing the analysis of samples containing high levels of dissolved solids while maintaining outstanding precision and reproducibility. Its flexible operating modes and automated analytical capabilities support both routine elemental analysis and advanced research applications.

Key Features

  • Triple quadrupole (ICP-MS/MS) technology for enhanced selectivity and accurate interference removal.
  • Advanced collision/reaction cell with multiple gas modes for efficient elimination of spectral interferences.
  • High sensitivity with ultra-low detection limits for trace and ultra-trace elemental analysis.
  • Excellent matrix tolerance for the analysis of complex samples containing high total dissolved solids (TDS).
  • Reliable quantification of challenging elements such as silicon (Si), phosphorus (P), sulfur (S), and other difficult analytes.
  • High-throughput operation with automated method development and user-friendly analytical workflows.
  • Flexible operating modes supporting routine elemental analysis as well as advanced research applications.
  • Suitable for environmental, metallurgical, geological, biological, pharmaceutical, nanomaterial, and advanced materials research.
  • Compliant with international safety and performance standards for modern analytical laboratories.

Research Applications

  • Trace and ultra-trace elemental analysis of environmental, biological, and industrial samples.
  • Quantitative determination of major, minor, and trace elements in metals, alloys, ceramics, polymers, and composite materials.
  • Heavy metal analysis in water, soil, wastewater, and environmental monitoring studies.
  • Geochemical and mineralogical investigations for elemental composition and contamination assessment.
  • Pharmaceutical, biomedical, and clinical research involving elemental profiling and impurity analysis.
  • Quality assurance, process control, and regulatory compliance in industrial and research laboratories.
  • Food safety and agricultural studies, including nutrient and contaminant analysis.
  • Characterization of high-purity chemicals, electronic materials, and semiconductor-grade materials.
  • Isotopic analysis and isotope ratio measurements for geoscience, nuclear science, and forensic applications.
  • Nanomaterial characterization and elemental composition analysis.

Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscope (FESEM) with EDS and EBSD

(01 Nos)

The SIGMA 500VP Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscope (FESEM), equipped with Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS) and Electron Backscatter Diffraction (EBSD) systems, is a high-resolution analytical platform for advanced surface imaging, elemental characterization, and crystallographic analysis. The instrument combines field emission electron optics with variable pressure (VP) technology to deliver exceptional image quality across a wide range of conductive and non-conductive materials with minimal sample preparation.

The FESEM enables detailed investigation of surface morphology, microstructure, particle size, fracture features, coatings, thin films, and nanostructures at micro- and nanometer scales. The integrated EDS detector provides rapid qualitative and quantitative elemental analysis, while the EBSD system facilitates crystallographic characterization, including grain orientation, phase identification, texture analysis, grain boundary mapping, and strain evaluation. These capabilities make the system an indispensable tool for multidisciplinary research in materials science, metallurgy, mechanical engineering, nanotechnology, electronics, polymers, ceramics, geology, and biomaterials.

Key Features

  • High-resolution field emission electron optics for nanoscale surface imaging with excellent contrast and clarity.
  • Variable Pressure (VP) operation for imaging both conductive and non-conductive specimens with reduced charging effects.
  • High-vacuum and low-vacuum imaging modes to accommodate a wide variety of sample types.
  • Integrated Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS) for rapid qualitative and quantitative elemental analysis and elemental mapping.
  • Integrated Electron Backscatter Diffraction (EBSD) for crystallographic orientation mapping, phase identification, texture analysis, grain size measurement, and grain boundary characterization.
  • High-resolution secondary electron (SE) and backscattered electron (BSE) imaging for comprehensive microstructural evaluation.
  • Automated large-area imaging, particle analysis, and high-resolution image acquisition for research and industrial applications.
  • Suitable for characterization of metals, alloys, polymers, ceramics, composites, semiconductors, thin films, nanomaterials, geological specimens, and biological samples.
  • Supports advanced research in failure analysis, fracture mechanics, additive manufacturing, corrosion studies, coating evaluation, and materials development.
  • User-friendly software with automated imaging, analytical workflows, and data acquisition for efficient and reproducible analysis.

Research Applications

  • Surface morphology and microstructural characterization of engineering and functional materials.
  • High-resolution imaging of nanomaterials, nanoparticles, nanofibres, and thin films.
  • Elemental analysis and elemental mapping using Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS).
  • Crystallographic orientation mapping, grain size analysis, and phase identification using Electron Backscatter Diffraction (EBSD).
  • Fracture surface and failure analysis of metals, polymers, ceramics, and composite materials.
  • Characterization of additive manufactured components and assessment of printing defects.
  • Corrosion, oxidation, wear, and coating evaluation.
  • Microstructural analysis of welded joints, heat-treated alloys, and metallurgical specimens.
  • Characterization of semiconductors, electronic devices, and microelectronic components.
  • Analysis of biomaterials, geological specimens, catalysts, and advanced functional materials.

X-ray Diffractometer (XRD)

(01 Nos)

The Malvern Panalytical Empyrean X-ray Diffractometer (XRD) is a state-of-the-art, multipurpose analytical system designed for comprehensive crystallographic and structural characterization of materials. Based on non-destructive X-ray diffraction (XRD) principles, the instrument provides precise information on crystal structure, phase composition, lattice parameters, crystallite size, residual stress, texture, and thin-film properties. Its highly flexible optical configuration enables the characterization of a broad spectrum of materials, ranging from powders and bulk solids to thin films, nanomaterials, coatings, polymers, ceramics, metals, semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, geological specimens, and advanced functional materials.

The Empyrean XRD is equipped with advanced X-ray optics, high-performance detectors, and automated measurement capabilities, allowing researchers to perform both routine characterization and sophisticated structural investigations with exceptional accuracy and reproducibility. The integrated HighScore Plus software provides powerful tools for qualitative and quantitative phase analysis, Rietveld refinement, crystallographic analysis, structure solution, and comprehensive diffraction data interpretation, making the system an indispensable resource for academic research, industrial quality control, and advanced materials development.

Key Features

  • Non-destructive crystallographic characterization with high precision and reproducibility.
  • Multipurpose platform capable of analyzing powders, bulk materials, thin films, coatings, nanomaterials, and solid specimens.
  • Qualitative and quantitative phase identification using advanced diffraction analysis.
  • Determination of crystal structure, lattice parameters, crystallite size, microstrain, preferred orientation (texture), and residual stress.
  • High-resolution measurements for phase purity assessment, polymorphism studies, and structural evolution.
  • Thin-film characterization through X-ray Reflectivity (XRR), Rocking Curve Analysis, and Reciprocal Space Mapping (RSM).
  • Supports in-situ and non-ambient diffraction experiments for studying temperature- and environment-dependent material behavior.
  • Suitable for research in metallurgy, ceramics, polymers, composites, pharmaceuticals, semiconductors, batteries, nanotechnology, geology, catalysts, and energy materials.
  • Automated instrument alignment and flexible optics for rapid switching between multiple experimental configurations.
  • Integrated HighScore Plus software for comprehensive diffraction data analysis, including phase identification, Rietveld refinement, crystallographic indexing, structure visualization, cluster analysis, and quantitative phase analysis.

Research Applications

  • Crystal structure determination and phase identification.
  • Quantitative phase analysis and Rietveld refinement.
  • Residual stress and texture analysis of engineering materials.
  • Thin-film and multilayer coating characterization.
  • Crystallite size and microstrain evaluation.
  • Phase transformation and thermal behavior studies.
  • Battery, catalyst, semiconductor, and advanced functional material characterization.
  • Quality control and failure analysis of industrial materials.
  • Nanomaterial characterization and structural investigations.
  • Pharmaceutical polymorphism, mineralogical analysis, and geological studies.

60 MHz Bench-Top Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) Spectrometer

(01 Nos)

The Nanalysis NMReady Pro 60 MHz Bench-Top Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) Spectrometer is a compact, cryogen-free analytical instrument designed for rapid and non-destructive molecular structure characterization of liquid samples. Operating with a 1.4 T permanent magnet, the system eliminates the need for liquid helium or liquid nitrogen while delivering reliable, high-quality NMR spectra for routine analysis and research applications.

The instrument is capable of acquiring ¹H, ¹³C, and ¹⁹F NMR spectra, enabling the identification, structural elucidation, and purity assessment of a wide range of organic and organometallic compounds. Its user-friendly operation and rapid data acquisition make it an ideal platform for academic research, chemical synthesis, reaction monitoring, quality control, and teaching laboratories. The system supports researchers in chemistry, pharmaceutical sciences, materials science, polymers, and related interdisciplinary fields by providing valuable molecular-level information.

Key Features

  • Cryogen-free 60 MHz permanent magnet system with low maintenance requirements.
  • Capable of acquiring ¹H, ¹³C, and ¹⁹F NMR spectra for liquid-state samples.
  • Non-destructive molecular characterization with rapid spectral acquisition.
  • High spectral stability and reproducibility for routine and research applications.
  • User-friendly software for automated data acquisition, processing, and spectral interpretation.
  • Suitable for structural elucidation, compound identification, purity assessment, and reaction monitoring.
  • Compact benchtop design requiring minimal laboratory infrastructure.
  • Supports teaching, academic research, industrial R&D, and quality assurance applications.
  • Safe and environmentally friendly operation without the need for cryogenic liquids.

Research Applications

  • Structural elucidation and molecular characterization of organic and organometallic compounds.
  • Identification and confirmation of synthesized chemical compounds.
  • Purity assessment and impurity profiling of chemicals and pharmaceutical intermediates.
  • Reaction monitoring and kinetic studies in organic synthesis.
  • Characterization of polymers, monomers, and specialty chemicals.
  • Analysis of fluorinated compounds using ¹⁹F NMR spectroscopy.
  • Quality control and product verification in chemical and pharmaceutical industries.
  • Academic teaching and training in molecular spectroscopy and analytical chemistry.
  • Research in medicinal chemistry, natural products, materials science, and chemical engineering.

Fourier Transform Infrared (FTIR) Spectrometer

(01 Nos)

The Bruker ALPHA II Fourier Transform Infrared (FTIR) Spectrometer is a compact, high-performance analytical instrument designed for rapid, non-destructive identification and characterization of materials based on their molecular vibrational signatures. The system provides accurate and reproducible infrared spectra for qualitative and quantitative analysis of a wide variety of organic, inorganic, and polymeric materials.

Equipped with a temperature-controlled DLaTGS detector, RockSolid™ cube corner interferometer, and an integrated Attenuated Total Reflectance (ATR) sampling module, the instrument enables direct analysis of solids, powders, liquids, films, fibres, and pastes with minimal or no sample preparation. The OPUS software platform offers automated spectral acquisition, processing, library searching, and quantitative analysis, making the system suitable for both routine testing and advanced research applications.

Key Features

  • Non-destructive molecular characterization using Fourier Transform Infrared (FTIR) spectroscopy.
  • Temperature-controlled DLaTGS detector for stable, high-quality spectral acquisition.
  • RockSolid™ cube corner interferometer ensuring excellent optical stability and long-term reliability.
  • Wide spectral range of 350–8000 cm⁻¹ for comprehensive molecular analysis.
  • Integrated ATR sampling module enabling rapid analysis with minimal sample preparation.
  • High signal-to-noise ratio for accurate detection of weak absorption bands.
  • High spectral accuracy and precision for reliable qualitative and quantitative analysis.
  • Compact benchtop design with USB and standalone operating capabilities.
  • Advanced OPUS software for automated data acquisition, spectral processing, library search, quantitative analysis, and report generation.
  • Suitable for routine quality control, research, teaching, and industrial applications.

Research Applications

  • Identification and characterization of organic, inorganic, and polymeric materials.
  • Functional group analysis and molecular structure determination.
  • Quality control and raw material verification in chemical, pharmaceutical, and polymer industries.
  • Characterization of polymers, composites, coatings, adhesives, and elastomers.
  • Analysis of pharmaceuticals, excipients, and drug formulations.
  • Investigation of biomaterials, natural products, and food samples.
  • Failure analysis, contamination identification, and material degradation studies.
  • Characterization of fibres, thin films, nanomaterials, and advanced functional materials.
  • Environmental analysis of pollutants and chemical contaminants.
  • Academic research and teaching in spectroscopy, chemistry, materials science, and chemical engineering.

Micro-Conical Twin Screw Extruder

(01 Nos)

The Thermo Fisher Scientific HAAKE™ MiniCTW Micro-Conical Twin Screw Extruder is a compact, laboratory-scale extrusion and compounding system designed for advanced polymer processing, material development, and formulation studies. The instrument is particularly suitable for research involving small sample volumes, requiring as little as 5 g or 7 mL of material, thereby minimizing material consumption while enabling efficient process optimization.

The system features co- and counter-rotating conical twin screws, allowing precise control over mixing, compounding, and extrusion of a wide range of materials, including engineering polymers, polymer blends, nanocomposites, biopolymers, pharmaceutical formulations, and functional composites. A programmable bypass valve and circulation mode provide adjustable residence time for improved material homogenization and process control. The integrated software continuously monitors key processing parameters such as torque, temperature, screw speed, and residence time, enabling real-time process analysis and reproducible experimental outcomes.

Key Features

  • Laboratory-scale conical twin screw extruder designed for material development and process optimization.
  • Processes sample quantities as low as 5 g or 7 mL, making it suitable for expensive and novel materials.
  • Co- and counter-rotating conical twin screws for efficient mixing, compounding, and extrusion.
  • Maximum operating temperature of 300°C for processing a wide range of thermoplastic and engineering polymers.
  • Adjustable screw speed ranging from 1 to 360 rpm for precise process control.
  • Programmable bypass valve and circulation mode for controlling material residence time and mixing efficiency.
  • Constant speed and constant torque operating modes for reproducible processing conditions.
  • Real-time monitoring of torque, temperature, screw speed, and process parameters through integrated software.
  • High-pressure extrusion capability of up to 200 bar for advanced material processing.
  • Suitable for polymer compounding, formulation development, and laboratory-scale extrusion research.

Research Applications

  • Development and processing of thermoplastics, engineering polymers, and high-performance polymers.
  • Fabrication and optimization of polymer composites and nanocomposites.
  • Compounding of fibre-reinforced polymer composites and functional materials.
  • Development of biodegradable and bio-based polymer formulations.
  • Processing of pharmaceutical formulations and drug delivery materials.
  • Preparation of polymer blends, masterbatches, and specialty compounds.
  • Material formulation and process parameter optimization for additive manufacturing feedstocks.
  • Investigation of rheological behavior, dispersion quality, and mixing efficiency.

Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) 3D Printing Facility

(05 Nos)

The Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) 3D Printing Facility at the Institute Instrumentation Center (IIC) provides advanced additive manufacturing capabilities for rapid prototyping, product development, functional part fabrication, and academic research. The facility comprises five (05) industrial and desktop-grade FDM 3D printers, including one (01) Bambu Lab X1E Combo, two (02) Bambu Lab A1 Mini Combo systems, and two (02) 3D Cubic CUB 6.0 large-format printers. Together, these systems offer a versatile platform for fabricating components ranging from small, high-precision prototypes to large engineering models.

The facility supports the processing of a wide range of thermoplastic materials, including PLA, PETG, TPU, PVA, ABS, and ASA, enabling researchers to manufacture complex geometries with excellent dimensional accuracy and surface finish. The Automatic Material System (AMS) integrated with the Bambu Lab printers facilitates automated multi-material and multi-colour printing, while the large-format CUB 6.0 printers enable fabrication of components up to 550 × 550 × 550 mm³. The facility is extensively used for research, education, product development, and proof-of-concept validation across multiple engineering disciplines.

3D Scanner

(01 Nos)

The RangeVision NEO 3D Scanner is a high-precision, structured-light optical scanning system designed for the accurate, non-contact digitization of physical objects into detailed three-dimensional (3D) digital models. The system combines high-resolution optical imaging with automated scanning capabilities to capture complex geometries, surface textures, and dimensional features with excellent accuracy and repeatability.

Equipped with a 2 MP colour camera, an automated rotary table, and RangeVision ScanCenter NG software, the scanner supports multiple scanning modes, including automatic alignment using markers, turntable-based scanning, and geometry-based alignment. The system enables rapid generation of high-quality 3D models for reverse engineering, dimensional inspection, product development, digital archiving, and additive manufacturing. Its user-friendly workflow and non-contact measurement technique make it suitable for a wide range of academic, industrial, and research applications.

Key Features

  • Non-contact structured-light 3D scanning for accurate digital reconstruction of physical objects.
  • High scanning accuracy of up to 0.05 mm with a 3D resolution of 0.13 mm.
  • Scanning area of 200 × 150 × 150 mm with support for objects ranging from 3 cm to 1.2 m in size.
  • Integrated 2 MP colour camera for capturing geometry along with realistic surface colour information.
  • Automated rotary table with a 20 kg load capacity for efficient and repeatable scanning.
  • Multiple alignment modes, including marker-based, turntable-assisted, and geometry-based registration.
  • USB 3.0 connectivity for high-speed data transfer and efficient workflow.
  • Integrated RangeVision ScanCenter NG software for scan acquisition, alignment, mesh generation, and model processing.
  • Suitable for reverse engineering, dimensional inspection, digital documentation, and additive manufacturing applications.

Research Applications

  • Reverse engineering of mechanical components and engineering products.
  • Generation of CAD models for product redesign and manufacturing.
  • Dimensional inspection and geometric accuracy evaluation.
  • Quality control and comparison of manufactured components with CAD models.
  • Digital preservation and documentation of artefacts, cultural heritage objects, and biological specimens.
  • 3D model generation for additive manufacturing and rapid prototyping.
  • Surface geometry acquisition for finite element modelling (FEM) and simulation.
  • Product development, industrial design, ergonomics, and customized component design.
  • Biomedical and anatomical model digitization for research and education.
  • Academic research in mechanical engineering, manufacturing, robotics, materials engineering, and digital engineering.
